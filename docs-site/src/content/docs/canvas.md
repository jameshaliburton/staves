---
title: "Explore and change the canvas"
description: "Inspect first, then move, connect or group the work."
---
- Hover a job for a compact preview. Select it to inspect or edit; open it to explore its tasks.
- Drag a job between others to preview its placement. Drop over a job to choose placement or group the pair in an epic.
- Moving work between a human and an agent opens a handover review. Check the responsibilities and conditions before accepting.
- Drag a connection between jobs to define the handoff. Connections on focus keeps other lines quiet.

Grouping and collapsing help you navigate. They do not erase individual roles, tasks or connections.
